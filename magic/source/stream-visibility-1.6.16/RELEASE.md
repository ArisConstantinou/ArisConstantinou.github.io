MAGIC 1.6.16 reduces repeated HEARTH flame clipping work while preserving the original ray stops, range, damage, graphics and assets. Unobstructed dual outlets now need two visibility queries instead of 68. Obstructed outlets use the same sampled distances with a binary search; near-parallel tolerance transitions and nozzles below the floor retain linear probing.

All 351 unit/integration checks pass. The new operation-budget checks fail on 1.6.15 and pass after the change. Differential coverage includes 6000 deterministic 2D/3D collision cases, floor/near-parallel edge cases and 48 positions/directions against the actual castle colliders. Native boarding and firing were exercised. Identical scene CPU batches took approximately 36.25ms before and 4.95ms after per 200 clipping updates; this isolated CPU improvement is not an FPS multiplier.

The same-scene before/after captures were visually inspected. Stream data is identical. Strict image hashes also differed for unchanged A/A controls; consecutive GPU render comparisons showed only 0–16 changed channels of 1,538,784, with maximum difference 1/255. The explicit image criterion permits one 8-bit level in at most 0.01% of channels and records unchanged controls. Failed strict-hash attempts are retained, not represented as passing tests. No graphics quality setting changed.
