LAST CALL CAPTAIN — HUMAN CHARACTER ASSETS

Michelle.glb
Source: https://threejs.org/examples/models/gltf/Michelle.glb
Repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Michelle.glb
Character: Michelle, Mixamo / Adobe.
Included source animation: SambaDance (not used in the game) and TPose.
The game retargets the Soldier idle/walk/run animations to this character while
preserving Michelle's proportions. A shader selectively varies blue fabric;
the textured face and skin are not globally tinted.

Soldier.glb
Source: https://threejs.org/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
Repository: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb
Character: Mixamo / Adobe (Vanguard / Soldier).
Included animations: Idle, Walk, Run, TPose.
Source attribution shown by three.js:
https://threejs.org/examples/webgl_animation_skinning_blending.html
