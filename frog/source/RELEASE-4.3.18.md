# FROG4.3.18

Original looping pond music with separate persistent level; hop takeoff and landing cues; final5-second battle countdown with sound and a Fight banner. Manual mobile aiming/firing retained.

Practice mode uses the regular frog and controls with two moving, non-shooting frog targets, quick target respawns and infinite player ammunition. Entering/leaving practice never replaces the saved normal round.

Real per-frog round standings track battle shots/hits, accuracy, knockouts/deaths, pickups and destroyed bases. Best50 completed normal rounds persist locally by player name; training and incomplete legacy telemetry are excluded. Points:100/base,10/KO,1/accepted fly hit. Surviving bases tie when a player exits by elimination; a winner is shown only when one base remains. Older saved games continue with a partial-statistics notice.
