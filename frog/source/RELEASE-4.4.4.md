# Βαλτομαχίες4.4.4

The imported cartoon bodies bypassed the earlier extended-leap pose, and their baked Jump/DoubleJump clips only offset their folded feet by0.30metres. They visibly travelled through the air in a seated crouch. A body-frame, bind-aware pose layer now extends both hind limbs and trails the webbed feet during takeoff/flight, retracts them for landing and reaches the forelimbs towards contact. It uses each body's actual joint lengths and parent transforms; no bone stretching or common assumed rest axis. Ordinary movement hops, big/double jumps, directional dash and wall launch use the pose. Wall-held contact, hit/KO, mounted poses and class attack limb performances remain owned by their existing systems. Ordinary hops gain a small flight-pivot lean; the existing missile-style big-jump/wall-launch attitude remains.

Same original assets, textures, rigs, geometry, gameplay physics, cached loading, resolution and render LOD/culling. The fix adds CPU skeletal posing, not new model downloads or render passes. Research reference: Li et al.2021, https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.7589 (preparation, hindlimb-extension takeoff, flight and forelimb-first landing). The longer extension timing is an authored adaptation for this game's long leaps.

The new catapult was anchored at its operator, putting its chassis centre1.35m away from the room centre and through the entrance. A shared transform now centres the actual chassis independently of turret heading and moves the operator, grips, collision footprint, launch socket and preview consistently. Dismount uses the clear entrance approach; navigation treats padding-boundary goals consistently. Original mesh/texture bytes remain unchanged.
