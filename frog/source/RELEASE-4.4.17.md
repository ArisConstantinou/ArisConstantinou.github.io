# Βαλτομαχίες 4.4.17

Heavy aimed rock arcs previously increased takeoff velocity above the class limit to clear a tall rock. Heavy now rises at most1.25m on the first jump and1.85m total above takeoff with a second jump or dash. Reachable perches and fresh-jump departure remain supported; other character balance is retained.

The title menu briefly opens without frogs, then complete approved character portraits arrive from the browser edges and land on their buttons. Heavy arrives from below, grips the button rim, makes two efforts and pulls up. The entrance runs once per page load, cancels cleanly on navigation/resize, and respects reduced motion. Static cards replace requests for the old animated menu WebPs; original WebPs and all original models remain preserved.

The experimental in-game ledge pull-up is inactive and excluded from the runtime: its original forearm/hindfoot mesh coupling fails whole-motion visual acceptance. Its complete local source/tests and diagnostic evidence remain preserved for repair. The menu pull-up does not claim to implement in-game climbing.

Joint validation covers the full source suite, native keyboard and CDP touch, desktop/portrait/landscape menu entry and controls, cancellation/reduced motion, original asset hashes, current bundle scope, old-tab save/settings upgrade and exact public deployment. Mobile viewports use a desktop GPU; this release does not certify physical-phone FPS or a2x speedup.
