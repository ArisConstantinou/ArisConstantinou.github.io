# Βαλτομαχίες 4.4.14

Wall destination input now survives catapult control installation. Manual wall jumps follow pointer/touch aim; short confirmation clicks launch without firing a fly. Automatic grips also consume contextual clicks. A new solid wall grip supplies a new launch ceiling, allowing successive frog wall jumps without flips.

Wall and catapult guides use 4.5px colored strokes with dark borders, direction chevrons and clearer landing markers. Buffers are reused and guide shaders prewarm during the existing load phase.

Normal jump rises: heavy1.9m, medium2.3m, skinny2.6m; double-jump/dash share a3.1m rise ceiling above takeoff. Low rock landings and slope hops remain accessible. The existing match clock displays the20-second warmup without a central card or duplicate countdown.

Original game models, approved branding, camera choice, UI editor and save format are unchanged. The menu retains4.4.13 assets. Skinny menu knee/ankle correction and heavy ledge pull-up remain unfinished and are not included. Physical iPhone FPS and2x performance are not certified.
