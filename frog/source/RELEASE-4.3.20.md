# FROG 4.3.20

The preparation timer is a compact unboxed number at the top right, beside the radar. Both early preparation time and final 5–1 leave the center clear; round timing and audio remain unchanged.

Mobile controls trial Fortnite-style manual Tap Anywhere: drag the free right-hand world area to aim/turn, release without firing, then tap briefly for one shot. The separate Fire button still supports held bursts and drag aiming. The left control retains directional hopping. Settings offer Tap Anywhere, the previous dedicated Fire method, and the existing optional automatic mode. The mobile trial is applied once; later choices persist. Desktop controls are unchanged.

Tap classification rejects long holds, movement beyond 6 CSS pixels (including out-and-back drags), cancellation, other fingers, and mounted catapult input. Accepted intent is buffered for one simulation tick or short shot cooldown, consumed by one actual player shot, and cleared by pause or conflicting actions. No automatic targeting is added.
