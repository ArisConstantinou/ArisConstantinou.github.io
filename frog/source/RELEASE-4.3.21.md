# FROG 4.3.21

The three menu frogs now perform articulated character animation while their hands remain planted on the real button rims. The original approved transparent textures are unchanged. Texture-clipped lower jaws and independent eyelids animate locally; no whole-character translation, rotation or fade is used.

Green and orange frogs open their mouths and shoot actual illustrated flies along timed trajectories. The receiving frog reacts after contact. The purple frog tracks a fly, opens its mouth, extends a deforming curved tongue to it, retracts with the fly attached to the tip and swallows. Individual fly wings flap independently of body flight.

The lightweight native SVG rig runs at30 updates/second only while the main menu is visible. It pauses during gameplay, Settings and leaderboards, and honors reduced motion with a stable pose. Static original artwork remains the fallback until textures decode. No dependencies, new downloads or raster assets are required. Gameplay, controls, saves, timer, audio, training and records are preserved.

Rig coordinates are authored for the two current1774×887 textures; future artwork replacements require adjusting the anatomy masks and contact points.
