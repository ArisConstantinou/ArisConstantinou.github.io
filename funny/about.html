It's a parody.
Not a leaked transcript.

Every answer and speech bubble attributed to the cartoon Tibo and Sam is invented. The characters do not reproduce their actual words, reveal their thoughts, or establish their motives. Self-serving answers are rewarded as a game mechanic, not described as factual or morally correct.

The users are the hosts. You control Tibo, the contestant. Sam is a fictional backstage supporter. Neither individual nor OpenAI is affiliated with or endorses this game. The artwork is AI-generated caricature with animated game layers; it is not a photograph or recording.

Background, outside the game

The questions fictionalise concerns discussed by the creator and in the previous version: short reset notice, different timezones, saved resets, unclear expiry, access to features, reliability, and value. Scenarios are not claims about every current account. Some resets have been banked; a reset can arrive while someone is asleep; a simple refill from 70 to 100 adds 30 points rather than taking 70 away. Those nuances belong here, not in a punchline.

This update changes the game and dialogue, not the research. The earlier research was a limited sample, not a full analysis of all replies. Original discussions and official documentation can be consulted separately:

Privacy & sound

No account, ads, analytics, external embeds, or AI calls are used in gameplay. Progress and best score are saved only in your browser. Sound is optional and synthesized; no real person's voice is recorded or cloned. All points and prizes are fictional. Version 4.0.0.
